Schoolbag — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Schoolbag ("we", "us", "our") provides an AI-powered study platform for South African matric (CAPS), NCV, and NATED students at schoolbag.co.za. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding it, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Study profile: student type (matric, NCV, or NATED), grade or level.
  • Subscription information: your plan (Free, Matric Pro, or TVET Pro) and any promo code used.
  • Usage data: which subject, topic, and content type (notes, quiz, flashcards) you generate, and when — used only to track your own progress and improve the product.
  • Content you submit: topics, text, or material you provide to generate study content.

2. What We Do NOT Store

  • We do not permanently store the AI-generated notes, quizzes, or flashcards themselves — only that a session happened, not the content generated.
  • We do not store your answers to practice quiz questions.
  • We never see or store your payment card details — payments are processed entirely by PayFast, our third-party payment processor.

3. How We Use Third-Party Services

To generate study content, the topics and material you submit are sent to Google Gemini (Google's AI service) for processing, subject to Google's own privacy terms. Payments are handled by PayFast. We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

4. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

5. Data Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing, never in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

6. Children's Privacy

Schoolbag is intended for high school and TVET college students. Where a user is a minor, we recommend a parent or guardian review this policy with them.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
