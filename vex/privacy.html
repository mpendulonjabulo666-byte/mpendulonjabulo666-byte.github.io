Vex Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 (study activity, mood check-ins money entries and profile pictures added)

Vex is a study and personal assistant app. This page says, in plain words, what Vex collects, why, who else sees it, and how you stay in control. It follows South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

The short version. Your information is private to your account. We do not sell it, and we do not show adverts. You can leave almost every question blank. You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Vex is run by Njabulo Dlamini in Newcastle, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. For any privacy question or request, email mpendulonjabulo666@gmail.com.

3. What we do not collect

  • We never ask for, and never store, your banking details or other passwords. If you type one into a chat, Vex tells you not to share it.
  • Vex does not record or keep audio. When you talk to Vex, your words are turned into text on your phone, and only the text is used.
  • "Hey Vex" is off until you switch it on. When it is on, Vex listens for the phrase only while the app is open on your screen, using speech recognition that runs on your phone. Nothing it hears is sent anywhere or kept. If your phone cannot do this on the phone itself, "Hey Vex" stays off.
  • Vex does not track your location in the background and does not read your screen unless you turn screen help on yourself and tap to ask.

4. Who else handles your information

To run Vex we use these services. They process your information only to provide their part of the service.

  • Supabase: stores your account and your data, protected so each person can only reach their own rows.
  • Render: runs the Vex server that talks to the AI and to Google for you.
  • Google Gemini: the AI that writes Vex's replies. What you type to Vex, and the study profile Vex needs for that reply (including how you study, such as when you score best and which topics are still weak, worked out from your own practice results), is sent to it. While Vex uses Google's free AI service, Google may keep and review this content to improve its products. Please do not put private secrets into chats with Vex.
  • Google sign-in (if you choose "Continue with Google"): Google tells us your name and email address so we can make your account.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: a quick check that you are a person and not a bot, when you sign in or sign up.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the web version of Vex.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising. We may share information if the law requires it.

5. How we protect it

  • Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each person can only read and change their own rows. This is enforced by the database itself, not just the app.
  • Passwords are hashed. Google access keys are encrypted. The sign-in on your phone is kept in its secure storage.
  • Vex never sends an email, deletes your data, or changes your calendar without you tapping to approve it.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise it. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

To work with no data, the phone app keeps a copy of your study lists (tasks, calendar, exams, flashcards, notes and progress) on your phone for up to 7 days. Chats, mood and money are never kept in it, and signing out removes it.

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your chats yourself in Profile. To delete your whole account and everything in it, tap Profile, then Delete my account: it happens straight away. If you cannot sign in, email us (section 8) and we will do it within 30 days.

7. Children and students under 18

Vex is made for students, and many are under 18. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to read this page with you and agree before you use Vex. Under POPIA a parent or guardian can give this agreement for you. A parent or guardian can email us at any time to see, correct or delete a child's information. We do not use information from anyone under 18 for advertising.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to how we use it or withdraw your agreement at any time. Email mpendulonjabulo666@gmail.com from the email address on your account, so we know it is you. Most things you can also change yourself in Profile.

If you are not happy with how we handled your information, you can complain to South Africa's Information Regulator at inforeg@justice.gov.za.

9. Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before it applies to new information we collect.
